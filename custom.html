Custom / request

Bring your
own idea.

Rare Threads welcomes requested designs — a message, a drawing, a detail you've been picturing. Every request is discussed and evaluated directly with Mbali before it's accepted. Not everything can be made, and that's said upfront rather than promised away.

How it works
01

Message your idea

Describe what you're picturing on WhatsApp — a message, a drawing, a detail, a reference.

02

Discuss feasibility & price

Mbali talks it through with you — what's possible, timeline, and cost.

03

Agree & confirm

Once you're both aligned on the design and price, payment terms are agreed and confirmed.

04

Made & delivered

Your piece is made, then collected in Diepsloot or delivered locally where practical.
