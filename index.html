What rare means

Not hard to find.
Hard to repeat.

Rare isn't about scarcity for its own sake. It's about individuality — clothing that defines you rather than dresses you. Every piece is built to tell a story about who's wearing it, not to follow whatever's trending.

No filler prints. Every design starts with an idea — a drawing, a quote, a stitch detail, a pocket that means something — before it becomes a garment.

Stitching

Construction as expression, not just technique.

Pockets

Unusual placement and shape, considered on purpose.

Messages

Motivational and kasi-rooted words, visible on the garment.

Story

Every piece carries an idea before it carries a print.

The founder

A rare breed, always begged to differ.

Mbali Shongwe grew up in Soweto and creates today from Diepsloot — intwana yase kasi. What started as cutting and ripping his own clothes as a kid became a first tracksuit made from scratch, and then a calling.

"When I create, I feel like myself" — Mbali

Behind the threads

One person. One machine. One dream.

Rare Threads is made at home, on a sewing machine, with handwork where it's needed. There's no factory and no studio yet — and that's part of the story, not something to hide.
