What rare means

Being yourself.
Being impossible to copy.

Rare means being yourself. Having the confidence to follow your own heart, trust your perspective and create without letting people decide who you should be.

Rare Threads is built around Kasi style, life experiences, music, creativity and ideas that deserve to be seen. Every piece is a chance to express something about yourself.

The founder

A rare breed, I BEG TO DIFFER.

Mbali Shongwe grew up in Soweto and now creates from Diepsloot — intwana yase kasi. His story started with cutting and changing clothes before he ever imagined making them from scratch.

What started as an idea became a calling. Today, Rare Threads is his way of putting his perspective, creativity and Kasi story into something people can wear.

"When I create, I feel like myself." — Mbali

Behind the threads

One person. One machine. One dream.

Rare Threads is being built from the ground up in Diepsloot — with one machine, ideas, hard work and the determination to see where this talent can go.

There is no big factory behind the brand. There doesn't need to be — not yet. This is where the dream begins.
